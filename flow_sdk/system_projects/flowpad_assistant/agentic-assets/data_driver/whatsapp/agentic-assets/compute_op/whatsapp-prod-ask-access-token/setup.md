A permanent token, so the bot does not stop after 60 days:

1. **Business Settings → Users → System users → Add** (role *Admin*).
2. **Assign assets**: your app (full control) and your WhatsApp Business Account (full control).
3. **Generate new token** for your app, expiry **Never**, permissions `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management`.
