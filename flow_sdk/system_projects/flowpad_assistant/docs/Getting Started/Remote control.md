# Remote control

**Remote control lets you open this machine from the Flowpad hub, and it is on only after you confirm with your fingerprint, for the time you choose.** It lives in the **Remote control** panel that drops from the menu at the top right of the app. An **access key** is a passkey — the fingerprint, face or PIN prompt of your browser or phone — that you add to your Flowpad account once and that the machine then accepts.

- **Off unless you turned it on.** Turning it on asks for your passkey every time. There is no way to leave it on without one.
- **It turns itself off.** You choose how long it stays on: 1 hour (the default), 3, 10 or 24 hours. At the end of that time it is off again. **Lock now** turns it off at once.
- **The machine decides.** The machine checks your fingerprint answer itself, against the key it accepted. A key you added for this machine is accepted by itself; any other key of yours waits on the machine for 10 minutes until you press **Allow**, and is dropped if you do not.
- **One setting per machine.** It covers the whole machine, not one project.
- **Not available** while you are signed out, in private mode, or on a machine signed in as an agent.

## Adding an access key

1. On the machine, open the menu at the top right of the top bar (its last button) and choose **Remote control**. The Remote control panel opens under the menu button.
2. In the panel, press **Add access key**. Your browser opens the hub's Machines page and the **Add access key** window.
3. Flowpad emails you a 6-digit code; type it and press **Continue**.
4. Your browser asks you to create a passkey. Confirm it.

The key is now listed under **Access keys**, named after what keeps it (for example "iCloud Keychain"), with a short code such as `3f9a c271`. The same code is shown in the machine's Remote control panel, so you can tell the two are the same key. You can **Rename** or **Remove** a key on the hub page at any time; you can have up to 10. A key removed there stops being accepted by your machines; when a machine's last accepted key goes, its remote control turns off.

## Turning it on

1. On the machine, open the menu at the top right → **Remote control**.
2. Beside **Stay on for**, choose **1 h**, **3 h**, **10 h** or **24 h**.
3. Press **Turn on** (or use the switch at the top of the panel). Your browser opens the hub's Machines page with the **Turn on remote control** window, and asks for your passkey.
4. Confirm with your fingerprint. The page says "Fingerprint confirmed" and goes back to Flowpad by itself; if it does not, press **Open Flowpad**.

Back in the app the panel says **on**, with your email, the time left and **Lock now**. While it is on, a glowing icon sits in the top bar; point at it to see the time left, click it to open the panel.

If you cancel the passkey prompt, the page says "Nothing was turned on." and you can press **Confirm with passkey** to try again.

## Turning it off

- Press **Lock now** in the panel, or switch off the switch at the top of the panel. It is off at once.
- Or do nothing: at the end of the time you chose it turns itself off. The panel then says so, for example "Off. It turned itself off after 1 hour. Turning it on needs your fingerprint again."

## Where else it shows

- **The menu row** itself says "on" or "off".
- **From the hub page:** once a key is added, the page says "One more step" with an **Open Flowpad** button. It opens Flowpad on the machine with the Remote control panel already open.
- **On the hub's Machines page** the machine's card says where it stands: "No access key · remote control off", "Access key set · remote control off" or "Access key set · remote control on". The card only reports; turning on happens on the machine.

A key you added somewhere else shows in the panel as "new", with its code and the minutes left; press **Allow** to accept it.
