passkey-infrastructure

The generic access-key piece every protected feature reuses, shown for its first kind, a passkey. Part 1 registers a passkey once - the device makes a key pair and the hub saves only the public key as an AccessKey. Part 2 is one confirmation - any verifier (a hub action or a machine) issues a challenge, the device signs it after a fingerprint, and the verifier checks the signature with the public key.
